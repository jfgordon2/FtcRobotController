# BIOBUZZ Letter AprilTag targets: where they come from and how to print them

- Publisher: FIRST, 2026–2027 BIOBUZZ field resources.
- Official listing: https://ftc-resources.firstinspires.org/ftc/field
- Download: https://ftc-resources.firstinspires.org/ftc/field/apriltag-us
- Local copy: `biobuzz-apriltags-letter-v1.pdf`, unchanged, eight landscape US Letter pages.
- Field mounting: https://ftc-resources.firstinspires.org/ftc/field/eventfieldguide

FIRST's artwork remains FIRST's artwork. The Meeting 5 plan and worksheet pages are
team teaching material. `scripts/build_meeting5_print_pack.py` puts four handout
pages in front of the eight original sheets to make
`output/pdf/meeting5-shooter-auto-print-pack.pdf`. If FIRST publishes new target
sheets, download them again, replace the local copy, and rebuild the packet.

## Which pages to print

| Original pages (right, left) | Packet pages (right, left) | Name to choose in INIT | IDs left to right |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1, 2 | 5, 6 | BLUE AUDIENCE | 38, 39, 40, 41 |
| 3, 4 | 7, 8 | BLUE SCORING | 42, 43, 44, 45 |
| 5, 6 | 9, 10 | RED AUDIENCE | 34, 35, 36, 37 |
| 7, 8 | 11, 12 | RED SCORING | 30, 31, 32, 33 |

Meeting 5 uses RED AUDIENCE: it is the red CELL that faces up when a match starts.

## Print and check

Print Actual Size / 100%, single-sided. Measure a black square after printing: it
must be 3.25 inches. Keep the white borders. Join the matching left and right sheets
at their printed center marks, not at the paper edges, with the label strip below
the tags. Tag centers are 3.75 inches apart within each sheet, 5.50 inches apart
across the join, and 13.00 inches from the first to the last.

Do not redraw, screenshot, or rescale the target pages. The original PDF has hidden
layers holding the other clusters, so the builder copies the whole original
document and inserts the handout in front, which keeps the right tags visible.
After rebuilding, look at all twelve pages before printing.

## Wall layout used in Meeting 5

The tape rectangle is 20 inches wide with its bottom edge 53.5 inches and top edge
65.5 inches above the floor. Those are the heights of the upward CELL opening in
manual Figure 9-10. The tag row is centered below it with the top of the black
squares 2 inches under the bottom tape, close to where the tags sit under a real
CELL. A flat wall is a stand-in: the real opening and tags are tilted 30 degrees,
so camera ranges and launcher speeds are measured again on a real hive.
